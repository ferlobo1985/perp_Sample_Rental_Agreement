RENTAL AGREEMENT
Sample document for practice. Fictional landlord, tenant and address.

1. The parties
This agreement is between Maple Court Properties (the "Landlord") and Alex Sample (the "Tenant"). It is for the apartment at 12 Example Street, Apartment 4B, Sampletown (the "Apartment").

2. Length of the agreement
The agreement starts on January 1 and ends on December 31 of the same year (12 months).

3. Rent
The monthly rent is $1,200. It is due on the 1st day of each month, by bank transfer.

4. Late payment
If the rent is not paid by the 5th day of the month, the Tenant pays a late fee of $60, plus $10 for each extra day until the rent is paid.

5. Security deposit
The Tenant pays a security deposit of $1,200 before moving in. The Landlord may use it to pay for damage to the Apartment, other than normal wear and tear.

6. Utilities
The Tenant pays for electricity and internet. The Landlord pays for water and building maintenance.

7. Rules for the Apartment
No smoking is allowed inside the Apartment. Pets are allowed only with written permission from the Landlord. The Tenant may not rent the Apartment to other people (no subletting). Quiet hours are from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.

8. Visits by the Landlord
The Landlord may enter the Apartment to inspect it with 24 hours' notice. In an emergency, the Landlord may enter at any time without notice.

9. Leaving early
If the Tenant leaves before December 31, the Tenant must pay an early leaving fee equal to two months of rent ($2,400), and the security deposit will not be returned.

10. Renewal
If neither side gives written notice 60 days before the end date, the agreement renews automatically for another 12 months, and the rent increases by 5%.

11. Signatures
By signing below, both sides accept this agreement.

Landlord: ______________________   Tenant: ______________________
Date: ________________   Date: ________________
